### SWHR2-R-0014 · Order notifications disabled unless enabled at deployment

#### SWHR2-R-0014.01 · No notification is enabled at deployment
- **SWHR2-C-0046** (integration, approved, v1) With no notification enabled, approving and completely fulfilling an order sends no customer e-mail
  - Preconditions: Deployment config sets none of the approval, confirmation or completion notification switches (shipped defaults). Mail transport replaced by a capturing test outbox. Order 1001 PENDING for customer email c@example.com.

  - Step 2: Fulfil all lines of order 1001 so it becomes COMPLETED.

  - Step 4: Read the test outbox.
  - Expected: The outbox holds no e-mail to c@example.com: no approval, confirmation or completion message.

  - Preconditions: Deployment config enables the confirmation notification only. Capturing test outbox. Order 1002 with two lines, APPROVED, customer email c@example.com.

  - Step 2: Drain the mail queue.
  - Step 3: Read the test outbox.
  - Step 4: Read the effective notification switch values.
  - Expected: Exactly one e-mail is captured: a confirmation e-mail to c@example.com. The approval and completion switches still read as disabled.

### SWHR2-R-0062 · Asynchronous e-mail delivery from a queue

#### SWHR2-R-0062.01 · One request produces one e-mail
- **SWHR2-C-0054** (integration, approved, v1) One queued request for a@example.com produces exactly one e-mail with subject "Order Status: 1001"
  - Preconditions: Empty mail queue. Capturing transport.

#### SWHR2-R-0062.02 · Producer does not wait for delivery

  - Preconditions: Transport stub whose send blocks until the test releases it. Notifications enabled.
  - Step 1: Run an order-processing step that notifies a customer (e.g. approve an order).
  - Step 2: Check whether the step has returned while the transport send is still blocked.
  - Step 3: Check the mail queue contents, then release the transport.
  - Expected: The step returns (order is APPROVED) while the send is still blocked, and the e-mail request is on the mail queue; the e-mail is sent only after the transport is released.
