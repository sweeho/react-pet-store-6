### SWHR2-R-0125 · Supplier inventory screen display and update marking

#### SWHR2-R-0125.01 · Inventory is listed
- **SWHR2-C-0041** (e2e, approved, v1) Display Inventory lists each stock record with existing quantity, empty new-quantity entry and unmarked update marker
  - Preconditions: Stock records seeded: EST-1 = 40, EST-2 = 0, EST-3 = 12. Supplier signed in on the supplier home screen.
  - Step 1: Choose Display Inventory.
  - Step 2: For each listed row, read the item, the existing quantity, the new-quantity entry and the update marker.
  - Expected: Exactly three rows are listed: EST-1 shows 40, EST-2 shows 0 and EST-3 shows 12. On every row the new-quantity entry is empty and the update marker is unmarked.

#### SWHR2-R-0125.02 · Mixed marked and unmarked rows
- **SWHR2-C-0042** (e2e, approved, v1) Only marked rows are saved: EST-1 marked becomes 30, EST-2 unmarked stays 0
  - Preconditions: Stock records EST-1 = 40 and EST-2 = 0; supplier on the inventory screen.
  - Step 1: Enter 30 in EST-1's new-quantity entry and mark its update marker.
  - Step 2: Enter 15 in EST-2's new-quantity entry and leave its marker unmarked.
  - Step 3: Choose Submit.
  - Step 4: Reopen Display Inventory (or read the stock records).
  - Expected: EST-1's quantity is 30 and EST-2's quantity is still 0; the 15 entered for EST-2 is not saved.

### SWHR2-R-0126 · Supplier inventory screen batch submission

#### SWHR2-R-0126.01 · Several rows changed in one submission
- **SWHR2-C-0043** (integration, approved, v1) One submission with two marked rows saves both together and leaves the unmarked row unchanged
  - Preconditions: Stock records EST-1 = 40, EST-2 = 0, EST-3 = 12.
  - Step 1: Send a single inventory update submission with EST-1 new quantity 50 (marked), EST-2 new quantity 20 (marked) and EST-3 unmarked.
  - Step 2: Read all three stock records.
  - Step 3: Repeat with a submission where EST-2's new quantity is invalid (e.g. a negative number, or a stock record deleted before submit) to check the save is all-or-nothing.
  - Expected: After the valid submission EST-1 = 50, EST-2 = 20 and EST-3 = 12, all from one request. After the failing submission no row has changed, so EST-1 does not get its new value on its own.

### SWHR2-R-0149 · Supplier stock record per item

#### SWHR2-R-0149.01 · One stock record per item
- **SWHR2-C-0044** (integration, approved, v1) Creating a second stock record for EST-1 is refused and the existing record is unchanged
  - Preconditions: In-memory test database with a stock record EST-1 = 40.
  - Step 1: Insert a second stock record for item EST-1 with quantity 99 through the stock data layer.
  - Step 2: Read the stock records for EST-1.
  - Expected: The insert fails with a uniqueness error; exactly one EST-1 record exists and its quantity is still 40.

#### SWHR2-R-0149.02 · Quantity is mandatory
- **SWHR2-C-0045** (integration, approved, v1) Saving a new stock record for EST-2 without a quantity is refused
  - Preconditions: In-memory test database with no stock record for EST-2.
  - Step 1: Insert a stock record for item EST-2 with no quantity value (null or omitted).
  - Step 2: Read the stock records for EST-2.
  - Expected: The insert fails with a not-null or validation error, and no EST-2 record exists.
