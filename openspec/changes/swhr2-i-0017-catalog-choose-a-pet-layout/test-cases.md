### SWHR2-R-0041 · Home screen pet selection

#### SWHR2-R-0041.03 · Picture aligned with the names on a wide screen
- **SWHR2-C-0290** (e2e, approved, v1) On a wide screen the pet picture spans its panel and each pet region lines up with its name cell
  - Preconditions: The storefront is running. The browser viewport is wide enough that the pet selection picture's panel on /petstore is about 1400 px wide (more than 1000 px).
  - Step 1: Open /petstore.
  - Step 2: Measure the bounding boxes of the pet selection picture (alt "Pet selection map") and of its panel.
  - Step 3: Measure the bounding box of each of the five pet regions in the picture (Birds, Fish, Dogs, Reptiles, Cats).
  - Step 4: Measure the bounding box of each of the five name cells in the row below the picture.
  - Expected: The picture's left and right edges match its panel's within 2 px, so there is no blank space either side. Each pet region's left and right edges are within 2 px of the name cell below it.

#### SWHR2-R-0041.04 · Pet region selected on a wide screen
- **SWHR2-C-0291** (e2e, approved, v1) On a wide screen, clicking the centre of the Cats picture cell opens the CATS category
  - Preconditions: The storefront is running. The browser viewport makes the pet selection picture's panel on /petstore about 1400 px wide.
  - Step 1: Open /petstore.
  - Step 2: Measure the pet selection picture's bounding box.
  - Step 3: Click the point at 90 % of the picture's width and 50 % of its height (the centre of the fifth, Cats, cell).
  - Expected: The browser navigates to /petstore/category/CATS and the Cats category page is shown.
